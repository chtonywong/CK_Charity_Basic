What We Do

1. Financial Literacy (For Teens & Young Adults)

Many young people and low-income families struggle with money management because it isn't taught in standard schools. We offer practical tools[cite: 1]:

  • Workshops on budgeting, saving, and avoiding predatory lending[cite: 1].
  • Mentorship for at-risk youth facing financial stress and social isolation[cite: 1].
  • Teaching the ethics of wealth and generosity[cite: 1].

2. Focus on Dementia & Brain Health (For Parents & Caregivers)

Most dementia families rely on a caregiver network sharing the load, yet conventional systems often fail to recognize caregiver needs[cite: 1]. We provide[cite: 1]:

  • Caregiver support: respite services, training, and emotional support networks[cite: 1].
  • Educational workshops on brain health, early detection, and reducing stigma[cite: 1].
  • Support groups and exploring the link between oral health and cognitive decline[cite: 1].
